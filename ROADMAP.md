## Decisions and things tried

Key estimated from chords (`estimateKeyFromChords` in `src/lib/songKey.ts`): score over 24 keys = chords inside the scale (the root counts double) + time on the tonic chord (and less on the dominant) + first/last chord on the tonic; this tells a major key from its relative minor. Today the only chords in the data are those of the 3 scores (all with a curated key already): the estimator does not change the song page (which already shows "Key"/"Possible keys" where there is data, and the measured range), but in `validate` it reports when the score's key does not match the chords. On Your Song and Anna e Marco it matches, on E cerca 'e me capi it does not (D vs Bb). Limits: a song that modulates gets a single key; modes (Dorian, Mixolydian) are ambiguous, e.g. Wonderwall comes out D with a margin of 0. Not published in the UI because without chords there is nothing to estimate; the estimate from audio stays the one already tried (64%, not published).

- 2026-11-20 (Alessio, from the brief): writing lead sheets is dropped for now: the transcriptions were too imprecise and, without lyrics (forbidden for copyright), they make no sense. The single melody/bass staff is also on hold (decisions Q2/Q7 of DESIGN.md §20 not taken).

Artist's range → comfortable range: already covered, no code added. In the song page `KeySuggestion` compares the range measured from the recording (`measuredShift`, histogram by singing time; fallback on the voice type with `rangeShift`) with the comfortable range from *My voice*, proposes the transposition, writes the target key with the right spelling (`transposedKeyLabel`) and has the "Apply" button (or "✓ applied"); `VocalHistogram` shows the measured range next to the comfortable one. Edge cases covered in `src/lib/vocalRange.test.ts`. Only the data coverage remains open (161 songs without a matched video, item already in the list).

Ranking: the site is public, so the repo holds only percentiles (`mine.ts`), never counts or dates. Plays count on the Spotify recordings linked to the song (main link, versions, translations); never-played songs = 0, so even a single play already counts above the middle of the ranking (ties at average rank). Global = percentile of the best Spotify popularity among the recordings; without popularity the Mix uses only Mine. Genres: none invented, 109 artists without P136 → "No genre".

Instrument emoji: the data model had no instruments. Added optional `instruments` to `SpotifyVersion` and `YoutubeCover`; when missing, `src/lib/instruments.ts` derives it with explicit keywords in the channel name or the note (chitarra/guitar/12-string/nylon-string, piano, violin, drums, sax, ukulele). Nothing is inferred from the artist or the song. Covers by Allie Sherlock, Fabio Rodrigues and Paolo Pilo stay without emoji (unless the note names the instrument): the data is needed from Alessio, to put by hand in `instruments`.

Key suggestion from the measured distribution: among the transpositions from −24 to +24 semitones it picks the one that puts the most singing time (histogram by time) in the comfortable range, ties go to the smallest (upward first); without measured data it stays on the voice type. Discarded for now: also weighting the histogram by number of notes or penalizing notes outside the *full* range but not comfortable (possible, but a choice to make with use); the distribution does not tell registers apart (chest voice/falsetto), see the idea already in the list. The estimate stays approximate: the measured recording can be in a different key than the chart.

Automatic suggestion from the vocal range (Mi2–Sib4 by default): the logic was already there (`measuredShift`, fallback on `rangeShift` from the voice type); in this step only edge-case tests (tie → upward, piece wider than the range, exact fit), suggestion shown also when the key is unknown (semitones only) and "✓ applied" instead of the Apply button when the transposition is already in use. Algorithm unchanged: minimizes singing time outside the range, ties go to the smallest absolute shift. Limit solved right after: the spelling of the label (e.g. "La#" instead of "Sib") followed the original key.

Spelling of transposed keys: now decided by the target key (major keys F, Bb, Eb, Ab, Db, Gb with flats, the others with sharps; minor keys follow the relative major), not by the original one. Applies to the key label, the suggestion, the preferred-key list and the chords in the transposed view (same `preferFlats`). Gb/F# major: Gb chosen (consistent with Eb minor with flats); F# minor with sharps. Song data not touched.

Long design document (musical representation, rendering, transposition, vocal range; partly outdated, see CLAUDE.md): [`docs/DESIGN.md`](docs/DESIGN.md).
